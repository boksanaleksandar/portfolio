About me

The person behind the pixels.

I'm Aleksandar Boksan, a self-taught product designer from Serbia. My journey started in 2017 when I began my career as a Graphic Designer. Since then, I've been freelancing and working with companies across the world, enjoying the collaborative process with clients.

I find inspiration in the unique stories each person brings. Great design should be invisible — guiding users effortlessly, solving real problems, and looking good doing it.

Currently based in Istanbul, Turkey, working on product design for local and international clients. When I'm not designing, I'm exploring the city or picking up a new language.
