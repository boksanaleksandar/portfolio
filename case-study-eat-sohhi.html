The problem

One habit, split across three apps.

The app is designed for busy business professionals in Qatar who have limited time to focus on diet planning and meal preparation. By answering a few registration questions, the app gathers essential user preferences to build a personalized meal plan and calculate daily calorie intake.

The goal was to enable quick food ordering in just a few clicks — freeing up time for users to work out at home or the gym with the support of guided video tutorials.

The approach

Three habits, one home screen.

Food, nutrition and fitness treated as three tabs feeding the same daily number: how well today went.

01 — Nutrition

A daily target that explains itself.

On the home screen, the user can see their daily nutritional intake and how much more they need to consume. They can browse or order food, with sorting tailored to preferences set automatically during registration.

Reminder — the user gets notified about which zone they're in regarding their daily nutritional intake targets.

  • Live macro ring with protein / carbs / fat split
  • Weekly calendar to track streaks, not just single days
  • Contextual reminders instead of silent tracking
02 — Ordering

From "what fits my macros" to a plate at the door.

Food is filterable by protein, carbs, fish or salads, each dish tagged with its nutritional breakdown so ordering never breaks the tracking upstream. Fulfilment is handled through Talabat, Qatar's established delivery network.

  • Meals pre-tagged with calories and macros before you order
  • Filter and sort by nutritional goal, not just cuisine
  • Talabat handles delivery so the order still lands hot
03 — Fitness

TrainSohhi turns extra calories into a reason to move.

Video tutorials are created by certified trainers specifically for users who are busy and don't have much time to work out. What sets the app apart is that after five orders, the user unlocks a free training section — a feature deliberately kept free because charging for it didn't align with the app's vision.

  • Filter workouts by body part, equipment or level
  • Step-by-step instructions with video for every move
  • Ordering habits unlock free workouts — food and fitness reward each other
