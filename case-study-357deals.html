The problem

A directory people opened once, then forgot.

One of the key challenges faced during the app redesign was addressing the poor, user-unfriendly UX of the previous version. The original interface had issues like cluttered sorting and ineffective screen displays, making navigation difficult for users.

The goal was to create a more intuitive and accessible experience by restructuring these components into a cleaner, more efficient layout.

The approach

Discovery that invites browsing.

The home screen rebuilt around the way people actually explore Qatar's dining and retail scene.

01 — Discovery

Categories and stories, not just a search bar.

What sets the app apart is its comprehensive functionality, with everything well-organized and conveniently displayed in one place. Loyalty card tags were added directly onto products that offer bonus points, making it easy to spot rewarding purchases — alongside a feature that displays the total number of available stores at any given moment.

  • Location-aware home feed with rotating offers
  • Story-style highlights for time-sensitive deals
  • Curated collections group merchants by occasion, not just category
02 — Loyalty

A digital stamp card that works everywhere.

A dedicated screen showcases the loyalty card, illustrating how customers earn points through the app. Every time a customer purchases a product included in the loyalty program, they get the opportunity to earn valuable rewards.

  • Branded loyalty card per merchant, one shared wallet
  • Stamp grid makes progress toward a reward visible at a glance
  • Redeem in-store with a merchant code — no extra hardware
03 — Account

Profile and history that stay out of the way.

One of the new screens introduced in the redesign is OTP Verification, prompting users to enter a code during registration. A feature was also added to the user profile enabling people to view the history of their past purchases.

  • Purchase history linked directly to loyalty progress
  • Short, single-purpose sign-up and account screens
  • Merchant directory and language settings tucked into a side menu
